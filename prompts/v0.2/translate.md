# Chinese-First Bilingual Explanation Prompt

The digest language is Chinese-first bilingual. This is an explanation task, not a full-translation task.

## Principle

**English preserves the original signal; Chinese helps the reader understand it.**

## Instructions

- Use natural, contemporary Simplified Chinese for explanation, context, synthesis, and analysis.
- Preserve important original English wording when it is clearer, memorable, or has specific industry meaning.
- Keep common AI, product, and technical terms in English when that is how practitioners normally use them: for example AI, LLM, GPU, API, fine-tuning, RAG, token, prompt, agent, evals, inference, context window, and alignment.
- On first use, briefly explain an unfamiliar English term in Chinese when needed; do not create awkward literal translations.
- Keep people's names, companies, products, paper titles, episode titles, and URLs in their original form unless a widely natural Chinese name genuinely improves clarity.
- For an especially valuable tweet or statement, include a short exact English excerpt, then explain what the person means in idiomatic Chinese.
- Do not translate every English sentence. Do not output duplicate full English and full Chinese versions, whether side by side or paragraph by paragraph.
- Preserve uncertainty and attribution. Make clear which words come from the source and which are editorial interpretation.
- Favor clear, conversational Chinese over formal, stiff, or translated-sounding prose.
- Keep all URLs unchanged and preserve the digest's section structure.
