# Blog Editorial Analysis Prompt

Turn the supplied AI company blog post into selective editorial notes for a Personal AI Learning & Intelligence Digest.

## Extract the signal

- Name the blog and exact article title.
- Lead with the concrete announcement, result, method, argument, or change.
- Preserve specific numbers, benchmarks, limitations, and conditions when present; do not detach a result from its stated context.
- Identify a product, feature, research finding, technical pattern, or policy change clearly.
- Include one short exact English excerpt only when the original wording carries unusual value.

## Explain, do not merely summarize

In natural Chinese, explain:

- what happened or what the author claims
- what it actually means for an intelligent non-engineer
- why it may matter
- the underlying assumption or tradeoff when useful
- what remains uncertain, promotional, or unproven
- any concept that could become a useful piece of long-term AI literacy

Distinguish the article's claims from your interpretation. Do not present company framing as independently verified fact.

## Beginner Bridge

Assume the reader is smart but may be early in learning AI. If an unfamiliar concept is necessary to understand the article's main signal, do not define it with other unexplained terms. Start from a concrete situation or problem, explain the problem in plain Chinese, introduce the standard English term, make the causal chain visible, and then return to the article's claim and significance.

Use this selectively. Do not turn every announcement into a tutorial or flatten the technical substance. Explain only the prerequisite needed for today's item; if deeper foundations would overload the note, flag them as a possible Rabbit Hole.

## Style and integrity

Write Chinese-first while retaining standard English technical/product terminology. Use natural conversational Chinese rather than compressed expert shorthand or dictionary-style definitions. Do not output a full English version plus a full Chinese translation. Avoid filler and generic AI-news phrasing.

Use only the supplied article content. Include the direct article URL. No direct URL means omit the item.
